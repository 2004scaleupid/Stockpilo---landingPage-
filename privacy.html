02

What data we process

2.1 Account data

  • Email address and password. Passwords are stored by our database provider (Supabase Auth) only as a salted hash; we never see them in plain text.
  • Google Sign-In. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your email address, name and profile picture with us (OAuth scopes openid email profile). We do not receive your Google password.
  • Profile (profiles table): username, full name, avatar (uploaded image stored in our avatars storage bucket, or the URL of your Google picture), email-verification status, account creation and last sign-in time.
  • Onboarding answers you choose to give: investing experience level, how you heard about us / referral source.
  • Settings: language, currency, refresh interval, summary length, dashboard layout, notification preferences (email notifications, earnings, news, market events, price alerts, AI insights), and flags that record whether you have seen the tour or a trial message.
  • Acceptance of the Terms and this Privacy Policy: we record which version of the Terms you accepted and when, timestamped by our server, in a record you can read but not change (terms_acceptances). For email sign-up this is the ticked checkbox, recorded when the account is created; for Google sign-up it is continuing with Google under the notice that doing so means you agree. The record is kept while you have an account and deleted with it.
  • Security and session data held by Supabase Auth: sessions and refresh tokens, and the IP address and browser user-agent recorded with sign-in events.

2.2 Content you create in the Service

  • Watchlist (user_stocks): the tickers you follow.
  • Holdings (user_stock_lots): ticker, quantity, buy price, buy date and currency of positions you enter manually. We do not connect to any broker or bank account.
  • Investment notes ("theses") (user_stock_thesis and its history): the text you write about a stock, its tags and the price when saved.
  • Pilo agent instructions (pilo_agents, agent_instructions, agent_runs): the free-text instruction you give your agent, the plan compiled from it and a log of each run.
  • Alerts and saved items: price/volume alert settings, earnings alerts, saved Risk Lab scenarios and their alert preferences, and the Congress members and company insiders you follow (user_followed_people).
  • Push notification devices (push_subscriptions): if you turn on push notifications, a label for the device and the push address your browser issues for it (see sections 3 and 4).
  • Derived portfolio data we compute from your watchlist and holdings: portfolio metrics (holding count, sector breakdown, average beta), daily portfolio snapshots and briefs, and in-app notifications.
  • Feedback and support: thumbs up/down on AI answers and news summaries, feedback messages, and support requests (name, email, subject, message and any attachments you upload, stored in a private storage bucket).

2.3 AI usage data

  • What you type to our AI features (chat questions, investment notes sent for analysis, agent instructions) is sent to OpenAI to generate the answer — see section 4.
  • AI usage logs (ai_query_logs): your user ID, the ticker and type of query, response time and whether a cached answer was used. The message text is not stored in this log.
  • Earlier chat messages (chat_messages): the app no longer saves chat conversations, but messages saved by an earlier version stay on your account until you delete it. They are part of your download (section 8), also after a subscription has ended.
  • Usage counters: number of AI calls and chat messages today (to enforce daily limits), watchlist-change counters.
  • Generated outputs (ai_outputs): AI answers are stored so they can be reused and quality-checked. Some are linked to you (for example, suggestions generated from your agent instruction); others are keyed by a one-way hash of your user ID and input.

2.4 Payment data

  • If you subscribe, Stripe processes your payment. We receive and store your Stripe customer ID, subscription ID, plan, subscription status, trial dates and billing-period end date. We create the Stripe customer with your email address and your internal user ID.
  • We never receive or store your card number. Card details go directly to Stripe.

2.5 Technical data

  • Error reports sent to Sentry when the app crashes (see section 4).
  • Server logs: our backend records which function was called, whether it succeeded, how long it took, any error message, your internal user ID (when you are signed in) and whether the call came from a signed-in user, our own servers or an automated test browser (edge_fn_invocations). We use this to measure and limit how much market data each part of the app uses and to tell real use apart from our own testing. It never includes your email, the content of your requests or your IP address, and it is deleted automatically after 30 days. Our hosting providers additionally keep standard request logs, which include IP addresses.
  • Bot-check signals (Cloudflare Turnstile): your IP address, TLS fingerprint, browser user agent, and the site key and page address the check ran on (see sections 4 and 7).

What OpenAI receives. Only the text needed to answer a specific request:

  • AI chat: your question and recent messages of the conversation. (The server can also accept your watchlist and a portfolio summary as context; the current app does not send them.)
  • Investment-note analysis and note alerts: the text of your note and its ticker, including when our scheduled job checks your notes against the news.
  • Pilo agent: the text of your agent instruction, and tickers taken from your watchlist when the agent runs on schedule.
  • All other AI features (the stock page's AI analysis, stock summaries, market events, news summaries, earnings insights, the daily market recap and similar) send market data, news and tickers only — no information about you.

We do not send your name or email address to OpenAI. But what you type is sent as written, so please do not include personal information in chat messages, notes or instructions.

Market-data and content sources (no personal data). To show prices, news, and social posts we request data from Financial Modeling Prep, FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis), the U.S. EIA, GDELT, Alpha Vantage, Polygon, Reddit, and X and StockTwits (via RapidAPI). These requests are made **from our servers** and contain only tickers or data-series names — for social feeds, the tickers on your watchlist — never your name, email, user ID or IP address. Stock and theme photos come from Pexels and are served from our own storage. Ticker logos are requested by our Cloudflare Worker, not by your browser: your browser asks www.stockpiloapp.com/logo/…, and the Worker fetches the image from Financial Modeling Prep, Parqet or Twelve Data using only the ticker, and keeps a copy at Cloudflare for up to a week. Those three providers receive the ticker and a request from Cloudflare's servers, nothing about you.

Content loaded directly by your browser. A few things are still requested straight from third-party servers, which therefore see your IP address and browser details (and, for the page you are on, a referring address as your browser sends it):

  • country flags (flagcdn.com) and website icons shown with notes (google.com);
  • company logos on the IPO page (logo.dev, and image addresses stored with a listing);
  • YouTube video thumbnails and the pictures that news publishers host with their articles;
  • the list of members of Congress (unitedstates.github.io) and rocket-launch times (Launch Library 2, ll.thespacedevs.com), fetched by the page that shows them;
  • the Cloudflare Turnstile script (challenges.cloudflare.com), only on the forms it protects.

None of these requests carries your account data.

Other recipients. Authorised administrators at Vebify OÜ can see account, subscription and support data and AI usage logs to run the Service. We may disclose data to authorities where the law requires it. We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising.

Cookies and browser storage

StockPilo does not use advertising or analytics cookies. We use browser storage that is needed to make the Service work:

  • Sign-in session (Supabase Auth): stores your login session so you stay signed in.
  • Session-only login (sessionStorage: stockpilo_session_only) and the "remember me" choice (stockpilo_remember_me, stockpilo_was_session_only): if you chose not to be remembered, you are signed out when you close the tab.
  • Google Sign-In safety values (sessionStorage: google_oauth_nonce, google_oauth_state), cleared after sign-in.
  • Your email address in localStorage (stockpilo_last_email, and stockpilo_pending_verification_email while you verify your email), to pre-fill forms. Both are removed when you sign out.
  • Terms acceptance in progress (sessionStorage: stockpilo_terms_google_pending) while you are redirected to Google, cleared after sign-in.
  • Preferences: language (stockpilo-language), theme, sidebar state (sidebar:state cookie), whether you've seen the tour (stockpilo_tour_seen_<your user ID>) or hints.
  • Other local settings: recent stock searches (stockpilo_recent_searches), whether you hid holding amounts (hide-holdings-amounts-v1), when you last dismissed the install prompt (sp-install-snooze-until) or an announcement (stockpilo_announcement_dismissed), and the last figures you saw for a stock (stockpilo_prev_stats_<ticker>).
  • Service worker (/sw.js): receives push notifications you turned on; it caches nothing.
  • Caches of market data and news summaries to make pages load faster (no personal data).

Security check (Cloudflare Turnstile): the sign-in and password-reset page (/auth), the Help Center contact form and the support chat load Cloudflare's security check. It does not set cookies on our domain. It keeps a temporary value in browser storage on challenges.cloudflare.com, Cloudflare's own domain, to run the check. Most visitors never see it; if Cloudflare is unsure, it shows a "Verify you are human" checkbox. See Cloudflare's Turnstile privacy addendum (https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/).

This marketing site (stockpilo.com) loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) and code libraries from cdnjs (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) directly in your browser, so those servers see your IP address and browser details when a page loads.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your data and get a copy of it.
  • Rectification — most profile data can be edited in Profile / Settings.
  • Erasure — you can delete your account yourself on your Profile page (Delete Account, then confirm). This cancels any active subscription immediately and deletes your profile, watchlist, holdings, notes, agent, alerts, feedback, support messages, AI usage logs, avatar and login. Exceptions: billing records at Stripe (legal obligation), error reports until Sentry's retention expires, and the hashed deletion receipt.
  • Data portability — download your profile, watchlist, holdings, notes, alerts, followed people, saved scenarios, chat messages and support messages yourself as a JSON file (Settings → Your data → Download). This also works after your subscription has ended; earlier chat messages are then read for you on our server. Files you attached to support requests are not in the download: email us for those or for anything else you gave us.
  • Restriction of processing and objection to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

To exercise a right, email privacy@stockpiloapp.com. We answer within one month. We may need to verify your identity first.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Estonia the Andmekaitse Inspektsioon (Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate), www.aki.ee — or with the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

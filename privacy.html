02

What data we process

2.1 Account data

  • Email address and password. Passwords are stored by our database provider (Supabase Auth) only as a salted hash; we never see them in plain text.
  • Google Sign-In. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your email address, name and profile picture with us (OAuth scopes openid email profile). We do not receive your Google password.
  • Profile (profiles table): username, full name, avatar (uploaded image stored in our avatars storage bucket, or the URL of your Google picture), email-verification status, account creation and last sign-in time.
  • Onboarding answers you choose to give: investing experience level, how you heard about us / referral source.
  • Time zone: your device's time zone, saved so push alerts arrive at a sensible hour. Our admins also see the country it points to and your local time, to understand where the service is used. It is only roughly where your device is, and we do not work it out from your IP address.
  • Settings: language, currency, refresh interval, summary length, dashboard layout, notification preferences (email notifications, earnings, news, market events, price alerts, AI insights), and flags that record whether you have seen the tour or a trial message, and which radar items and weekly briefs you have already seen or closed.
  • Acceptance of the Terms and this Privacy Policy: we record which version of the Terms you accepted and when, timestamped by our server, in a record you can read but not change (terms_acceptances). For email sign-up this is the ticked checkbox, recorded when the account is created; for Google sign-up it is continuing with Google under the notice that doing so means you agree. The record is kept while you have an account and deleted with it.
  • Security and session data held by Supabase Auth: sessions and refresh tokens, and the IP address and browser user-agent recorded with sign-in events.

2.2 Content you create in the Service

  • Watchlist (user_stocks): the tickers you follow.
  • Stock pages opened (stock_opens): which stock pages you opened, one entry per stock per day, deleted after 8 days. Together with watchlists it is used only as a count for "Followed by others": a stock is listed there once at least two other people watch it or opened it, and no one is ever shown who they are.
  • Profile (user_public_profiles, user_follows): the handle, display name and bio, and who follows you and whom you follow. Every account has a profile, and it is public by default: you can make it private, switch any part off or delete it in Settings at any time. An account made from 15 November 2026 gets its profile when it is created, with a generated handle and display name (for example member3f9a1c2b7d40, never your email or real name) and no bio, which you can change. Accounts made before that date got one on 15 November 2026, named after the username chosen in the app or with a generated name. Signed-in Premium members can search for profiles: public profiles and the StockPilo team by name or handle, a private profile only by its exact @handle. A public profile can be suggested in Who to follow; a private one is not, and other signed-in members reach it through its link, or through the follower lists of profiles you follow or that accept your follow. They can see your photo, display name, handle, bio, follower counts and how many stocks are on your watchlist (unless you switch the watchlist off). Your watchlist, the people, themes and charts you follow, the stocks you keep a price alert on (never the price) and the newest stories and posts you save (saved_articles, 20 at most) are shown only to followers you approve, or to every signed-in member if you make the profile public. Each of these parts can be switched off, and deleting the profile removes it and its follows. Followers who can see a part also get one in-app note a day when you add something to it (notifications, type people_activity); switching the part off or removing a follower takes those notes away. Holdings and alert prices are never shown to anyone else. A stock you share with a member (only one on your watchlist, only with someone you follow who follows you back) is kept as a bell note for them (notifications, type stock_share: your handle and the ticker, no message); each member can turn receiving shares off in Settings. Stories (from 16 November 2026): the row of circles on the People page and the dashboard shows, for 24 hours, what members you follow added under the parts they show (a stock on the watchlist, a person followed, an earnings bell, the last one only between members who follow each other), and filings by the Congress members and insiders you follow (from your own bell notes, for 3 days). A stock find you post (profile_stories: one stock from your watchlist, an optional line of up to 140 characters as you wrote it, and the price your app showed when you posted) is seen only by members you follow who follow you back, for 24 hours, and you can delete it at any time. They can react with 👀 or 🔥 (story_reactions); you get a bell note naming who reacted (notifications, type story_reaction), and only you see the counts. Which circles you have already watched is kept so the rings stay grey on every device (story_seen). Deleting your profile or account removes your stories, your reactions and those notes. An earnings bell you ring on the Earnings page (earnings_alerts: the ticker and the report day) belongs to the alerts part: it is listed on your profile until the company reports, and members you follow who follow you back see your photo on that company's earnings card. Switching the alerts part off hides your bells from both; you still get the reminder. A public profile's link also works for people who are not members (from 16 November 2026): anyone who opens it, signed in or not, sees your photo, display name, handle, bio, follower counts and watchlist size, the same header members see, and nothing else; the rest of the page is hidden until they join. Apps that show a preview of a shared link (WhatsApp, Messenger, X) show your display name, handle, bio and those counts. Search engines are asked not to index profile pages (noindex). A private profile's link shows only that the profile is not available.
  • Holdings (user_stock_lots): ticker, quantity, buy price, buy date and currency of positions you enter manually. We do not connect to any broker or bank account.
  • Investment notes ("theses") (user_stock_thesis and its history): the text you write about a stock, its tags and the price when saved.
  • Pilo agent instructions (pilo_agents, agent_instructions, agent_runs): the free-text instruction you give your agent, the plan compiled from it and a log of each run.
  • Alerts and saved items: price/volume alert settings, earnings alerts, saved Risk Lab scenarios and their alert preferences, the Congress members and company insiders you follow (user_followed_people; other members see only how many members follow a Congress member or insider, never who), the Theme Radar themes you follow (user_followed_themes; other members see only how many members follow a theme, never who), and the news articles you bookmark (saved_articles: link, headline, publisher, image link, tickers and the time you saved it).
  • Push notification devices (push_subscriptions): if you turn on push notifications, a label for the device and the push address your browser issues for it (see sections 3 and 4).
  • Derived portfolio data we compute from your watchlist and holdings: portfolio metrics (holding count, sector breakdown, average beta), daily portfolio snapshots and briefs, and in-app notifications.
  • Feedback and support: thumbs up/down on AI answers and news summaries, feedback messages, and support requests (name, email, subject, message and any attachments you upload, stored in a private storage bucket).

2.3 AI usage data

  • What you type to our AI features (chat questions, investment notes sent for analysis, agent instructions) is sent to OpenAI to generate the answer — see section 4.
  • AI usage logs (ai_query_logs): your user ID, the ticker and type of query, response time and whether a cached answer was used. The message text is not stored in this log.
  • AI cost records (ai_usage): the AI feature and model, the number of tokens used and, when you triggered the call while signed in, your user ID. Our admins see the estimated cost per person, to control what the service spends on AI. The text of your requests is not stored here.
  • Earlier chat messages (chat_messages): the app no longer saves chat conversations, but messages saved by an earlier version stay on your account until you delete it. They are part of your download (section 8), also after a subscription has ended.
  • Usage counters: number of AI calls and chat messages today (to enforce daily limits), watchlist-change counters.
  • AI credit records (ai_credit_events): one row for each AI credit you use, with the time and the feature, so our admins can see what used an account's daily credits.
  • Generated outputs (ai_outputs): AI answers are stored so they can be reused and quality-checked. Some are linked to you (for example, suggestions generated from your agent instruction); others are keyed by a one-way hash of your user ID and input.

2.4 Payment data

  • If you subscribe, Stripe processes your payment. We receive and store your Stripe customer ID, subscription ID, plan, subscription status, trial dates and billing-period end date. We create the Stripe customer with your email address and your internal user ID.
  • We never receive or store your card number. Card details go directly to Stripe.

2.5 Technical data

  • Error reports sent to Sentry when the app crashes (see section 4).
  • Problem reports: when something fails for you (the app crashes, a section does not load, an answer times out, checkout or sign-in fails), our own database records what failed, on which page, when, and your internal user ID, so our admins can fix it. Only admins can read them.
  • Server logs: our backend records which function was called, whether it succeeded, how long it took, any error message, your internal user ID (when you are signed in) and whether the call came from a signed-in user, our own servers or an automated test browser (edge_fn_invocations). We use this to measure and limit how much market data each part of the app uses and to tell real use apart from our own testing. It never includes your email, the content of your requests or your IP address, and it is deleted automatically after 30 days. Our hosting providers additionally keep standard request logs, which include IP addresses.
  • Online status: while you are signed in and using the app, your browser tells our backend every minute or so that you are active. We keep one timestamp per user (user_presence), overwrite it each time and delete it after a day. Our admins see who is online right now, with name and email, to understand how the service is used. It records no pages and no activity.
  • Visits and e-mail reads (user_events): one entry when you open the app (at most one a day), when you open the recap (one per recap), when the push-notification offer is shown and when you turn push on from it, and for the "Your minute" e-mail: that it was sent, that it was opened (the e-mail loads one tiny image from our own server) and that you followed a link in it back into the app (the link passes through our server first). Links to other sites, the settings link and unsubscribe are not counted. We use it only to see whether people come back and what brings them back, never for advertising, and it is not shared. Turning the e-mail off stops the e-mail entries; images switched off in your mail app record no opens.
  • Bot-check signals (Cloudflare Turnstile): your IP address, TLS fingerprint, browser user agent, and the site key and page address the check ran on (see sections 4 and 7).

What OpenAI receives. Only the text needed to answer a specific request:

  • AI chat: your question and recent messages of the conversation. (The server can also accept your watchlist and a portfolio summary as context; the current app does not send them.)
  • Investment-note analysis and note alerts: the text of your note and its ticker, including when our scheduled job checks your notes against the news.
  • Pilo agent: the text of your agent instruction, and tickers taken from your watchlist when the agent runs on schedule.

Market-data and content sources (no personal data). To show prices, news, filings and social posts, our servers request data from third-party sources, for example: Financial Modeling Prep, Alpha Vantage and Polygon (market data); FRED, the U.S. EIA, BLS, the U.S. Treasury and the Federal Register (economic and government data); the SEC (company filings), USAspending (federal contracts), and the U.S. House and Congress websites (members and their trade disclosures); GDELT and news publishers' own web pages (news); Wikipedia and Wikidata (background facts); and X (through twitterapi.io and the X API) and StockTwits (through RapidAPI) for social posts. These requests contain only tickers, names from public records, data-series names or article addresses — for social feeds, the tickers on your watchlist — never your name, email, user ID or IP address. Stock and theme photos come from Pexels and are served from our own storage. Ticker logos are requested by our Cloudflare Worker, not by your browser: your browser asks www.stockpiloapp.com/logo/…, and the Worker fetches the image from Financial Modeling Prep, Parqet or Twelve Data using only the ticker, and keeps a copy at Cloudflare for up to a week. Those three providers receive the ticker and a request from Cloudflare's servers, nothing about you.

Content loaded directly by your browser. A few things are still requested straight from third-party servers, which therefore see your IP address and browser details (and, for the page you are on, a referring address as your browser sends it):

  • country flags (flagcdn.com) and website icons shown with notes (google.com);
  • company logos on the IPO page (logo.dev, and image addresses stored with a listing);
  • YouTube video thumbnails and the pictures that news publishers host with their articles;
  • the list of members of Congress (unitedstates.github.io) and rocket-launch times (Launch Library 2, ll.thespacedevs.com), fetched by the page that shows them;
  • the Cloudflare Turnstile script (challenges.cloudflare.com), only on the forms it protects.

None of these requests carries your account data. Portraits of members of Congress are also loaded by your browser from unitedstates.github.io.

Other recipients. Authorised administrators at Vebify OÜ can see account, subscription and support data and AI usage logs to run the Service. We may disclose data to authorities where the law requires it. We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising.

Cookies and browser storage

StockPilo does not use advertising or analytics cookies. We use browser storage that is needed to make the Service work:

  • Sign-in session (Supabase Auth): stores your login session so you stay signed in.
  • Session-only login (sessionStorage: stockpilo_session_only) and the "remember me" choice (stockpilo_remember_me, stockpilo_was_session_only): if you chose not to be remembered, you are signed out when you close the tab.
  • Google Sign-In safety values (sessionStorage: google_oauth_nonce, google_oauth_state), cleared after sign-in.
  • Your email address in localStorage (stockpilo_last_email, and stockpilo_pending_verification_email while you verify your email), to pre-fill forms. Both are removed when you sign out.
  • Terms acceptance in progress (sessionStorage: stockpilo_terms_google_pending) while you are redirected to Google, cleared after sign-in.
  • Your last sign-in check (authgate:v1:<your user ID>): your username, plan and trial dates from your last visit, so pages open without waiting for them. Checked again at once, removed when you sign out and after 24 hours.
  • Your last news lists (saved-list:v1:…): the headlines from your last visit to News, including your watchlist's stories, so the page shows them at once while fresh ones load. Replaced on every visit, removed when you sign out and after 24 hours.
  • Preferences: language (stockpilo-language), theme, sidebar state (sidebar:state cookie), whether you've seen the tour (stockpilo_tour_seen_<your user ID>) or hints.
  • Other local settings: recent stock searches (stockpilo_recent_searches), whether you hid holding amounts (hide-holdings-amounts-v1), when you last dismissed the install prompt (sp-install-snooze-until) or an announcement (stockpilo_announcement_dismissed), and the last figures you saw for a stock (stockpilo_prev_stats_<ticker>).
  • Service worker (/sw.js): receives push notifications you turned on; it caches nothing.
  • Caches of market data and news summaries to make pages load faster (no personal data).

Security check (Cloudflare Turnstile): the sign-in and password-reset page (/auth), the Help Center contact form and the support chat load Cloudflare's security check. It does not set cookies on our domain. It keeps a temporary value in browser storage on challenges.cloudflare.com, Cloudflare's own domain, to run the check. Most visitors never see it; if Cloudflare is unsure, it shows a "Verify you are human" checkbox. See Cloudflare's Turnstile privacy addendum (https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/).

This marketing site (stockpilo.com) loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) and code libraries from cdnjs (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) directly in your browser, so those servers see your IP address and browser details when a page loads.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your data and get a copy of it.
  • Rectification — most profile data can be edited in Profile / Settings.
  • Erasure — you can delete your account yourself on your Profile page (Delete Account, then confirm). This cancels any active subscription immediately and deletes your profile, watchlist, holdings, notes, agent, alerts, feedback, support messages, AI usage logs, AI credit records, problem reports, avatar and login. Exceptions: billing records at Stripe (legal obligation), error reports until Sentry's retention expires, and the hashed deletion receipt.
  • Data portability — download your profile, watchlist, holdings, notes, alerts, followed people, saved scenarios, saved articles, push devices, Pilo agent instructions, chat messages and support messages yourself as a JSON file (Settings → Your data → Download). This also works after your subscription has ended; earlier chat messages are then read for you on our server. Files you attached to support requests are not in the download: email us for those or for anything else you gave us.
  • Restriction of processing and objection to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

To exercise a right, email privacy@stockpiloapp.com. We answer within one month. We may need to verify your identity first.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Estonia the Andmekaitse Inspektsioon (Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate), www.aki.ee — or with the authority in the EU country where you live or work.
